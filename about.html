About

Bridging hardware and useful software.

I’m Raunak Kushwah, an independent software and automation engineer based in Gurugram, Haryana.

Software for the people running the line.

I develop applications that connect shop-floor devices with operator screens, production records and business systems. My focus spans .NET desktop software, RFID workflows, PLC communication, camera integration and reporting.

The aim is practical: show operators what is happening, handle communication problems clearly and keep the production history easy to investigate.
