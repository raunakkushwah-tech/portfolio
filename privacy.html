Contact information

The inquiry form asks for your name, email, optional company and phone, service interest and project description. These details are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss a possible engagement.

How the inquiry works

The default form prepares a draft in your email application. It does not send your message automatically or store form entries on this website. You decide whether to send the email. Phone and WhatsApp links open the relevant service, whose own privacy terms apply.

Analytics preferences

Optional analytics is not enabled in this version. This website does not load Google Analytics or set analytics cookies.

If optional analytics is enabled later, it will load only after you choose Allow. Your preference is stored in this browser. You can change it using Analytics preferences in the footer. Contact form text and contact details are not included in analytics events.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. The website uses locally hosted styling, fonts and scripts rather than external font services.

Questions and deletion requests

To ask about information you have shared or request deletion of an inquiry, contact raunakkushwah.tech@gmail.com. Necessary correspondence may be retained while a project or related business obligation is ongoing.
